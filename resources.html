SEARCH-FIRST RESOURCE

Questions business owners ask before investing in SEO

SEO is a system of related entities, attributes, and actions. In simple terms: a business has goals, a website has SEO attributes, Google has search and map systems, and an SEO agency connects those pieces with measurable work.

01 • BEFORE HIRING AN SEO AGENCY

Understand the SEO service first

An SEO agency is a service provider; its services are the actions that improve a business website, local presence, and search visibility.

1. What does an SEO agency do?

An SEO agency improves a business website's search visibility. Its work can include keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, content structure, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, and performance improvements.

2. Do I need an SEO agency for my business?

A business may need SEO support when Google visibility matters but the owner lacks the time or skills to manage SEO. The need depends on business goals, competition, website condition, and budget.

3. How do I choose an SEO agency in Itahari?

Check the agency's services, process, communication, reporting, relevant experience, and completed work. Ask what will change, why it matters, and how progress will be measured.

4. What should I ask an SEO agency before hiring them?

Ask about the SEO strategy, target keywords, local SEO work, technical fixes, reporting, timeline, pricing, contract terms, and how the agency handles Google ranking uncertainty.

5. What SEO services does an agency provide?

Common services include SEO audits, keyword research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, local landing pages, citation work, speed optimization, and website maintenance.

6. Can an SEO agency help my business get customers from Google?

SEO can improve a business's visibility in relevant Google searches. Better visibility can bring qualified visits, calls, enquiries, map actions, or sales, depending on demand, competition, website quality, and execution.

7. Can an SEO agency work with my existing website?

Yes. An SEO agency can audit an existing website and improve its technical setup, page structure, content, internal links, metadata, speed, mobile experience, and local search signals.

02 • ITAHARI & LOCAL SEARCH

Local SEO questions for Itahari businesses

Local SEO connects a business entity, service entity, location entity, and Google Business Profile so search engines can better understand local relevance.

8. Are there SEO agencies in Itahari?

Yes, businesses can find SEO and digital marketing providers serving Itahari and the wider Sunsari area. Compare each provider's service scope, communication, portfolio, and process.

9. Is there a local SEO agency in Itahari?

Local SEO is offered by agencies and freelancers serving Itahari businesses. Local SEO work can include Google Maps visibility, Business Profile optimization, local keywords, citations, reviews, and location pages.

10. Can an SEO agency in Itahari work with businesses anywhere in Nepal?

Yes. SEO delivery is largely digital, so an Itahari-based agency can work with businesses in other cities and provinces. The strategy changes according to location, market, services, and competition.

11. How can my Itahari business rank on Google?

An Itahari business can improve visibility by matching useful pages to local search intent, improving technical SEO, publishing clear service information, building local relevance, and keeping its Google Business Profile accurate.

12. How can my business appear on Google Maps in Itahari?

A local business should have an eligible Google Business Profile with accurate information and relevant categories. Local relevance, proximity, prominence, website signals, and genuine reviews can also affect visibility.

03 • SEO MONEY & PRICING

SEO pricing questions for Nepal businesses

SEO pricing is a service attribute. Cost changes with scope, business size, competition, technical work, content needs, local SEO work, and ongoing support.

13. How much does SEO cost in Nepal?

SEO prices in Nepal vary by provider and scope. A small local campaign can require less work than a competitive national campaign because keywords, pages, content, technical work, and competition differ.

14. How much does SEO cost for a small business?

A small business may need a focused scope such as local SEO, key service pages, technical fixes, Google Business Profile work, and reporting. The price depends on the amount of work required.

15. How much does local SEO cost?

Local SEO pricing depends on location, competition, number of services, citation needs, Google Business Profile work, website changes, and reporting frequency.

16. What is included in an SEO package?

An SEO package may include an audit, keyword research, on-page optimization, technical fixes, content recommendations, local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, reporting, and maintenance.

17. Do SEO agencies charge monthly?

Many agencies use monthly retainers because SEO requires ongoing monitoring and optimization. Some providers also offer one-time audits, project work, or fixed setup packages.

18. Is SEO a one-time service or monthly service?

SEO can be both. A one-time audit is a fixed service, while ongoing SEO is usually monthly because rankings, competitors, content, and search demand change over time.

04 • RESULTS & EXPECTATIONS

What to expect from SEO

SEO is a process. Search visibility depends on website quality, relevance, competition, authority, location, and search engine systems.

19. How long does SEO take to work?

SEO takes time because pages must be crawled, indexed, evaluated, and improved through repeated optimization. The timeline varies by website condition, competition, search intent, authority, and completed work.

20. How long does it take to rank on Google?

There is no universal ranking time. A less competitive local keyword may move sooner than a highly competitive national keyword. Page quality, technical health, authority, and competition all matter.

21. When will I see SEO results?

Early results can include improved indexing, keyword movement, impressions, or clicks before strong lead growth appears. Useful measurement combines search visibility with business outcomes.

22. Can SEO increase my website traffic?

Yes. SEO can increase organic traffic when relevant pages become more visible for useful search queries. Traffic quality matters because relevant visitors are more valuable than unrelated visits.

23. Can SEO generate leads for my business?

SEO can support lead generation by connecting searchers with relevant service pages, local profiles, contact information, and conversion paths. Lead volume also depends on demand, offer quality, trust, and conversion experience.

24. Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a specific Google ranking position. Search results depend on many external systems, competitors, user intent, location, and changes in Google's search processes.

05 • LOCAL SEO & GOOGLE MAPS

Local search questions

Local SEO uses business, service, and location entities together so Google can better understand what a business offers and where it serves customers.

25. What is local SEO?

Local SEO improves a business's visibility in location-based searches. It connects the business entity with services, locations, website pages, Google Business Profile information, and other local signals.

26. How does local SEO help a small business?

Local SEO helps a small business become easier to find when nearby customers search for relevant products or services. It can support website visits, calls, directions, enquiries, and local discovery.

27. How do I rank my business on Google Maps?

Start with a complete and accurate Google Business Profile, the right primary category, correct business information, relevant services, useful website pages, genuine customer reviews, and consistent local signals.

28. How do I optimize my Google Business Profile?

Use accurate business information, a suitable category, clear services, current hours, helpful photos, a strong description, and genuine customer review activity. Website and local citation consistency should support the same business entity.

29. How can I get more Google reviews?

Ask real customers for honest feedback after a completed purchase or service. Make the request simple and timely, and never buy or create fake reviews.

30. Why is my business not showing on Google Maps?

Possible causes include an incomplete profile, wrong category, address or service-area issues, weak local relevance, limited prominence, profile restrictions, or policy problems. A local SEO audit can identify the issue.

06 • WEBSITE PROBLEMS

Why websites struggle in Google search

A website is a connected entity system: pages, titles, headings, content, links, structured data, technical settings, performance, and mobile experience work together.

31. Why is my website not ranking on Google?

The cause may be weak search intent matching, poor on-page SEO, technical indexing problems, low topical depth, limited authority, weak local relevance, or strong competitors. An SEO audit helps separate these causes.

32. Why is my website not getting traffic?

A website may receive little traffic when target keywords have low demand, pages are not visible in search, content does not match intent, or technical and local SEO issues limit discovery.

33. Why are my competitors ranking higher than my website?

Competitors may have stronger page relevance, better technical health, more complete content, stronger local signals, more links, greater authority, or better search-intent alignment. An SEO comparison can reveal the gaps.

34. Does my website need an SEO audit?

An SEO audit is useful when you want to identify ranking, indexing, technical, content, or conversion issues. It shows what the website has, what is missing, and what should be improved.

35. Can technical SEO problems stop my website from ranking?

Yes. Crawlability, indexability, canonical errors, broken links, duplicate pages, poor site structure, blocked resources, and other technical problems can reduce how effectively search engines access and understand a website.

36. Does website speed affect Google rankings?

Website speed is an important performance and user-experience factor. Faster pages can make a site easier to use, while poor performance can hurt user experience and page experience signals.

07 • COMPARING SEO OPTIONS

SEO agency, freelancer, DIY, or Google Ads?

The suitable option depends on the business entity, goals, budget, skills, time, market competition, and need for ongoing support.

SEO vs ADS

37. SEO vs Google Ads — which should my business use?

SEO builds organic search visibility over time, while Google Ads provides paid advertising placements when a campaign is active. Some businesses use both for different customer journeys.

AGENCY vs FREELANCER

38. Should I hire an SEO agency or freelancer?

An agency may provide a broader team and service range, while a freelancer may provide a more direct single-person relationship. Compare scope, expertise, communication, availability, reporting, and cost.

DIY vs EXPERT

39. Should I do SEO myself or hire an expert?

DIY SEO can work when a business owner has the time to learn and execute consistently. An SEO expert can reduce the learning burden and handle more technical or ongoing work.

SMALL BUSINESS

40. Is SEO worth it for a small business?

SEO can be useful for a small business when customers actively search for its products or services online. Value depends on search demand, competition, customer value, conversion rate, and other customer-acquisition costs.
