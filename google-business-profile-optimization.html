Core Guide

How to set up and optimize a Google Business Profile.

A Google Business Profile should make the business easy to identify, understand and contact. The most important information should describe the same real business across the profile, website and other legitimate local listings.

1. Start with the actual business

Use the real business name and accurate business information. The profile should represent the actual company, shop, office, professional or local service provider rather than being created around search terms.

2. Choose the right business category

The primary category should describe what the business fundamentally is. Additional categories should also accurately describe genuine parts of the business.

Example: A cafe should use an appropriate cafe or food-service category rather than selecting an unrelated category simply because it contains a popular keyword.

3. Complete the important business information

Customers should be able to understand the business from its core information without confusion.

Business Information What It Communicates
Business Name The actual name customers use to identify the business.
Business Category What type of business it is.
Address Where the business is physically located.
Service Area The locations served when applicable.
Phone Number How customers can contact the business.
Website The official website connected with the business.
Business Hours When customers can visit or contact the business.
Services What the business provides.
Products What the business sells when applicable.
Photos Visual information about the real business.
Reviews Customer experiences associated with the business.

4. Describe services clearly

Business services should match what the business really provides. For example, an SEO agency may provide local SEO, technical SEO, on-page SEO and Google Business Profile optimization.

These services should also connect naturally with the relevant service pages on the website.

5. Keep information consistent

Important business details should agree across the Google Business Profile, website and other legitimate business references.

Name, address and phone information are especially important for maintaining a consistent local business presence.

6. Use relevant photos and customer information

Photos should represent the real business. Useful images may include the storefront, interior, products, team, completed work or other relevant business activity.

Customer reviews should also be monitored and answered professionally and accurately.

7. Connect the profile with the website

The Google Business Profile and the business website should describe the same business and communicate consistent information.

Example relationship: Startup Nepal provides Local SEO. Startup Nepal serves businesses in Itahari and other applicable areas. The official website explains the services represented by the business profile.

8. Review the profile regularly

Business hours, services, websites, phone numbers, locations and other information can change. Regular profile review helps keep customer-facing information accurate.

FAQ

Google Business Profile questions.

What is Google Profile Setup and Optimization?

It is the process of creating, completing and improving a Google Business Profile so accurate business information is available to customers in Google Search and Google Maps.

What information should a Google Business Profile include?

Important information can include the business name, category, address or service area, phone number, website, hours, services, products, photos and customer reviews.

Is Google Business Profile part of local SEO?

Yes. A business profile is one part of a broader local SEO strategy. A local SEO strategy may also include the business website, local landing pages, citations, content and other local business information.

Should keywords be added everywhere in a business profile?

No. Business information should accurately describe the real business. Keywords should be used naturally when they accurately describe the business, service or topic.
